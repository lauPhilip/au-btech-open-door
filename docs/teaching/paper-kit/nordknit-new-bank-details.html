Nordknit · New bank details

Paper kit for the class. Fictional company, CC BY-SA 4.0. A family-owned clothing company in its fourth generation. The office, the warehouse and the dye works share one site in Sunds. A sewing factory in Lithuania makes the clothes. Customers in nine countries buy them in the company's own webshops.

Goal

The factory says Nordknit has not paid. Find out how the money went missing.

How to play on paper

Print one copy of the floor plan per team, one set of cards for the teacher and one note sheet per team. Walk around by pointing at rooms on the floor plan. When a team stands in a room and asks a question, the teacher reads the answer from the card. Teams write threats on their note sheet. After the walk, read the story events aloud, hand out the vote sheet, and use the answer key to debrief.

The story

Karin: Welcome back to Nordknit. This morning the factory in Lithuania called me.

Karin: They say we have not paid their big invoice. But Elena says she paid it yesterday.

Karin: Both of them are telling the truth. That worries me more than a simple mistake.

Karin: Please walk around. Talk to my people. Tell me what could go wrong.

Floor plan

Nordknit, Sunds, Denmark. Every team starts in the meeting room.

People

The teacher reads the question the team picks and then the italic answer. Cut along the dashed lines.

Karin

CEO, fourth generation · CEO office

“The factory is angry. Without their clothes we have nothing to sell.”

  1. How big is the invoice?Forty-eight thousand euro. It is the biggest payment of the autumn.
  2. Who checks a payment before it goes?Nobody. Elena has done it for years, so I trust her. I never look.

Mei

Webshop manager · Webshop team

“I only sell the clothes. The money side is Elena's world.”

  1. Do you hear from the factory?Sometimes. They write to the shared team address, and we pass it on to Elena.

Ahmed

Part-time IT · Server cupboard

“I'm here on Mondays and Thursdays. Today is Monday, so you are lucky.”

  1. How is the accounting mailbox protected?With a password. Elena chose it herself. We talked about a second step, but never did it.
  2. What is in this cupboard?The router, the Wi-Fi and a backup disk. The door lock broke in the summer.

Jana

Packing lead · Packing station

“Half of our new stock is stuck at the factory. I'm not happy.”

  1. What happens if the factory stops sending clothes?We run out of the best sellers in two weeks. Then the packing line stands still.

Elena

Accounting · Accounting

“I paid it yesterday. I even have the receipt from the bank.”

  1. Why did you pay it yesterday?The factory wrote that their bank had changed. They asked me to use the new account before Tuesday.
  2. Did you check the new account with anyone?I answered the e-mail and they confirmed it. There was no reason to doubt it.
  3. What do you do with paper invoices?They go in the tray on my desk. I take them home on Fridays when I need peace.

Tomas

Driver, shipping partner · Loading dock

“Morning. Same route as always. A few letters for the office today.”

  1. Who takes the post?I leave it at reception. If nobody is there, I put it on the desk in accounting.

Things

The line “look closer” is for teams that ask to look closer.

Invoice tray

Accounting

Paper and e-mail invoices waiting to be paid. On top lies the factory's big invoice.

Look closer: The invoice carries the bank account. Nobody compares it with an earlier invoice.

Accounting inbox

Accounting

Elena's e-mail. The newest message is from "the factory", about new bank details.

Look closer: The sender address is nordknit-factory.com. The factory's real address ends in .lt.

Visitor book

Reception

Visitors write their name and the time. Reception is empty before 8:30.

Router and Wi-Fi

Server cupboard

Connects the office, the warehouse and the guest Wi-Fi to the internet.

Look closer: The accounting mailbox has no second step when someone logs in from a new country.

Live order screen

Webshop team

Shows orders from all nine shops as they come in.

The day

Alert: The factory says the invoice is not paid. Read these out at the times shown, one every few minutes.

08:10 · Karin: The factory manager is calling me again. He sounds upset.

08:40 · Elena: The bank receipt says the money went to an account in another country.

09:10 · Ahmed: I looked at the mailbox log. Someone logged in from Romania last week.

09:40 · Jana: The factory has stopped our next delivery until they get paid.

Clues

Give a clue to a team that stands in the room for the first time after the day has started.

Accounting: The bank receipt shows an account in Romania. The factory's usual account is in Lithuania.

CEO office: Karin: the factory says it never asked to change its bank details.

Server cupboard: Mailbox log: a login from Romania, six days ago at 06:12.

Reception: Visitor book: 'Courier, letter for Elena, 07:50'. Reception was empty then.

Teacher only · answer key

A fake e-mail from "the factory" with new bank details was paid without a second check.
Where: Accounting inbox · Harm: change · Actor: online · Likelihood: 4

One person can pay any invoice alone. Nobody looks at the big ones.
Where: Invoice tray · Harm: change · Actor: online, employee · Likelihood: 3

The accounting mailbox has only a password. Someone who guesses it can read and send mail as Elena.
Where: Router and Wi-Fi · Harm: leak, change · Actor: online · Likelihood: 3

Paper invoices with bank details lie open on a desk. A visitor could copy or swap them.
Where: Invoice tray · Harm: leak, change · Actor: visitor, employee · Likelihood: 2

Reception is empty before 8:30, so a visitor can walk to the accounting desk.
Where: Visitor book · Harm: leak, change · Actor: visitor · Likelihood: 2

The server cupboard is unlocked. Someone could take the backup disk or unplug the network.
Where: Router and Wi-Fi · Harm: leak, stop · Actor: visitor, employee · Likelihood: 2

Results of the choices

A: The reply comes back in minutes and says yes. It is the same person who sent the fake. The money is gone.

B (best): The bank can stop part of the payment. The factory confirms the e-mail was fake. Elena changes the mailbox password.

C: The factory ships again. But Nordknit has paid 48,200 euro twice, and the first payment is still lost.

Debrief questions

  1. Which team found the Romanian account first? Which room gave it away?
  2. Elena did her job as she always does. Which small change would have stopped this?
  3. Several teams missed the mailbox password. Why is it easy to miss?
  4. If you could fix only one note before next month, which one, and why?
