Nordknit · Black Friday

Paper kit for the class. Fictional company, CC BY-SA 4.0. A family-owned clothing company in its fourth generation. The office, the warehouse and the dye works share one site in Sunds. A sewing factory in Lithuania makes the clothes. Customers in nine countries buy them in the company's own webshops.

Goal

Black Friday is in 3 days. Find what could stop an order reaching a customer.

How to play on paper

Print one copy of the floor plan per team, one set of cards for the teacher and one note sheet per team. Walk around by pointing at rooms on the floor plan. When a team stands in a room and asks a question, the teacher reads the answer from the card. Teams write threats on their note sheet. After the walk, read the story events aloud, hand out the vote sheet, and use the answer key to debrief.

The story

Karin: Welcome to Nordknit. My great-grandfather started this company with one knitting machine.

Karin: Black Friday is in three days. We expect ten times more orders than on a normal day.

Karin: Last year a small problem cost us a whole day of shipping. I can't let that happen again.

Karin: Please walk around. Talk to my people. Tell me what could go wrong.

Floor plan

Nordknit, Sunds, Denmark. Every team starts in the meeting room.

People

The teacher reads the question the team picks and then the italic answer. Cut along the dashed lines.

Karin

CEO, fourth generation · CEO office

“I care about one thing this week. Every parcel must leave on time.”

  1. What would hurt the company most?Losing our customers' trust. People buy from us because we are honest and reliable.
  2. Who can pay invoices?Elena in accounting. She has done it for years, so she does it alone.

Mei

Webshop manager · Webshop team

“Nine shops, one system. On Black Friday it gets very busy.”

  1. Where does the webshop run?A provider hosts it for us. We log in to a web page to manage it.
  2. Who can log in as admin?All four of us share one admin login. The password is on a note under my keyboard.
  3. What happens when there are many visitors?Last Black Friday the shop was slow for two hours. We never found out why.

Ahmed

Part-time IT · Server cupboard

“I'm here on Mondays and Thursdays. The rest of the week the IT runs itself. Mostly.”

  1. What is in this cupboard?The router, the Wi-Fi and a backup disk. The door lock broke in the summer.
  2. How many networks do you have?Office, warehouse and guest Wi-Fi. Drivers use the guest one when they wait.

Jana

Packing lead · Packing station

“Orders come in, labels come out, parcels go on the truck. That's my day.”

  1. How do labels get printed?The printer gets them straight from the webshop. IT put it on the guest Wi-Fi — it was easier for the drivers too.
  2. What if the printer stops?Then nothing ships. The truck leaves at ten, with or without our parcels.

Elena

Accounting · Accounting

“Invoices, salaries, suppliers. If it costs money, it passes my desk.”

  1. How do you pay the factory?They e-mail an invoice and I pay it. If their bank details change, they write to me.

Tomas

Driver, shipping partner · Loading dock

“Morning. I just need the parcels and a coffee.”

  1. How do you get into the building?The dock door is open in the morning. Nobody really checks. Everybody knows me.

Things

The line “look closer” is for teams that ask to look closer.

Label printer

Packing station

Prints shipping labels for every order. A small screen shows its status.

Look closer: Its admin page opens from any device on the guest Wi-Fi. The password is the factory default.

Live order screen

Webshop team

Shows orders from all nine shops as they come in.

Look closer: Orders arrive from the webshop provider over the internet.

Router and Wi-Fi

Server cupboard

Connects the office, the warehouse and the guest Wi-Fi to the internet.

Look closer: The guest and warehouse networks are not separated. Any guest device can reach the printer.

Visitor book

Reception

Visitors write their name and the time. Reception is empty before 8:30.

Invoice tray

Accounting

Paper and e-mail invoices waiting to be paid.

The day

Alert: Orders have stopped printing. Read these out at the times shown, one every few minutes.

08:04 · Mei: The shop is up and orders come in. But nothing has printed since 07:52!

08:20 · Jana: The printer screen says "paused by admin". None of us did that.

08:45 · Mei: The German shop is slow now. Customers are writing to support.

09:30 · Tomas: Truck leaves at ten. How many parcels do I get?

Clues

Give a clue to a team that stands in the room for the first time after the day has started.

Webshop team: 2,300 orders are waiting. The shop works, but labels don't print.

Packing station: The printer says "paused by admin". Its settings were changed at 07:51.

Server cupboard: Router log: a new device joined the guest Wi-Fi at 07:48.

Reception: Visitor book: 'Printer service, 07:40'. Nobody booked a printer service.

Teacher only · answer key

Anyone on the guest Wi-Fi can change or stop the label printer.
Where: Label printer · Harm: change, stop · Actor: visitor · Likelihood: 3

A stranger can walk in before reception opens and pretend to be a technician.
Where: Visitor book · Harm: leak, change · Actor: visitor · Likelihood: 2

The webshop can slow down or stop under ten times the normal traffic.
Where: Live order screen · Harm: stop · Actor: accident, online · Likelihood: 4

The admin login is shared and written on a note. Anyone could change prices or steal customer data. Nobody would know who did it.
Where: Live order screen · Harm: leak, change · Actor: employee, visitor · Likelihood: 3

The server cupboard is unlocked. Someone could take the backup disk or unplug the network.
Where: Router and Wi-Fi · Harm: leak, stop · Actor: visitor, employee · Likelihood: 2

A fake e-mail from "the factory" with new bank details could be paid without a second check.
Where: Invoice tray · Harm: change · Actor: online · Likelihood: 3

Results of the choices

A: Labels print again at 09:10. At 09:25 the printer is paused again.

B (best): Labels print from 09:05. The truck leaves full at 10:00.

C: Nothing more goes wrong — but no orders come in during the biggest sales day of the year.

Debrief questions

  1. Which team spotted the printer on the guest Wi-Fi? What made you look there?
  2. The fake visitor came in before reception opened. Which room should you have visited to see this coming?
  3. Several teams missed the shared admin password. Why is it easy to miss?
  4. If you could fix only one note before next Black Friday, which one, and why?
